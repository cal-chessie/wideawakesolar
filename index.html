Frequently asked

Still Have Questions?

Everything you need to know about going solar in Ireland. Can't find the answer? Talk to our team.

Ask Our Experts
How much will I actually save?+
Most customers cut bills by 60–80%. On a typical Irish bill of €200/month, that's a saving of €1,440–€1,920/year. Use our Bill Analyser above for a personalised calculation based on your exact usage.
What SEAI grants are available?+
SEAI offers up to €1,800 for domestic solar PV, plus an additional €300 if you add an EV charger — bringing the total to €2,100. We handle the full application, and grants are deducted from your installation cost upfront. No waiting for reimbursement.
How long does installation take?+
Most residential systems are installed in a single day. From first enquiry to activation is typically 4–6 weeks including survey, design, planning and installation.
Does solar work in Irish weather?+
Absolutely. Panels generate on daylight, not direct sunshine. Ireland's mild climate actually keeps panels cooler — improving efficiency vs hotter countries. Overcast days still produce meaningful power.
What is infrared heating and how does it save money?+
Infrared heaters are 100% electric, highly efficient, and can run directly off your solar generation. Replacing gas or oil heating with solar-powered infrared can cut your heating bill by up to 70%.
What warranty do you offer?+
25-year performance warranty on panels, 10-year on inverters, and a 10-year workmanship guarantee on all installations. We're here for the lifetime of your system.
